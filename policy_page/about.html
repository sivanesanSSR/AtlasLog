About Atlas Log

Atlas Log is built specifically for gym owners, personal trainers, and fitness center managers who need a fast, reliable, and privacy-focused solution to manage member records, subscription plans, and fee payments.

Designed with an offline-first architecture, Atlas Log ensures that all your business records stay stored directly on your device with zero cloud dependency and no unnecessary data tracking.
